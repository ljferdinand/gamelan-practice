Tone Prep

Turn recordings of a tuned percussion instrument into one clean, level-matched sample per bar. Trims to the attack, keeps the ring, cuts where another strike intrudes, and tells you which samples carry another bar's sound — because a contaminated sample plays that ghost under every note it ever sounds.

01Load recordings

Drop audio here, or

One file with every bar struck in turn, or one file per bar. WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC.

02Listen

Click a bar to hear the prepared sample. Bars run low to high and are sized the way real bars are. Oxidised bars are the ones flagged below.

03Check and label

barmeasuredlength leveltakessingle voice?

04Assign registers

Bars are grouped into octaves by position, counting up from the lowest. If the instrument's bottom octave is incomplete, say where its lowest bar sits inside a full octave — otherwise the grouping lands in the wrong place and a whole register can vanish. Check the result below against the instrument.

05Export

The bundle is one JSON file holding the tuning and the audio together. It is also a valid instrument profile, so the main app's Load saved instrument reads it directly. Individual WAVs are for editing elsewhere.
