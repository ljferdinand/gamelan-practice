Load a performance and transcribe it to follow along.
How it works: every gamelan is tuned to itself, so this tool measures your instrument's fundamentals from the samples you give it — no scale assumed. You supply the numbering from your own layout (highest note down); octaves come from pitch and show as a dot above (high), no dot (middle), or a dot below (low), so a repeated number stays unambiguous. Strikes in a performance are then snapped to your measured voices. A number in this colour is a strike that matched none of your voices well (an out-of-vocabulary sound — later this will help mark section breaks).
Audio in: WAV, MP3, M4A/AAC/MP4, FLAC (and AIFF where your browser supports it).
You can also write a score as plain text and have it played through your own samples — see how to write a score, which includes a live editor and a sample you can download and play.
